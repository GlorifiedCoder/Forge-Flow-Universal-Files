# ZCode Task — Store and Release Readiness

Review this specific app against the current official Apple App Store and Google Play policies that apply to its category, audience, countries, content, login methods, permissions, SDKs, payments and data practices.

Research official sources at execution time and record URLs plus date checked. Do not rely on stale policy text or generic templates alone.

Check app completeness, privacy policy, SDK/data inventory, App Privacy/Data Safety answers, account/data deletion, UGC moderation/report/block, age/child safety, consent/permissions, login requirements, payment rules, third-party IP/licensing, reviewer/demo access, target API/SDK, signing, screenshots, listing metadata, support/contact and regional requirements as applicable.

Create a project-specific compliance matrix. Mark every item `APPLIES`, `NOT APPLICABLE` with reason, `NEEDS RESEARCH`, `BLOCKED`, or `VERIFIED` with evidence. Do not submit or assert acceptance is guaranteed.
