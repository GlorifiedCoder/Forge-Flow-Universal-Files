# Official Store Policy Sources

Check the latest applicable text on the day of implementation and again before submission. Record the date, policy version, applicable clauses, decisions, and evidence in the project's store requirements matrix.

## Apple

- App Review Guidelines: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/
- App Store Connect Help: https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/
- App Privacy details: https://developer.apple.com/app-store/app-privacy-details/
- Sign in with Apple: https://developer.apple.com/sign-in-with-apple/
- Apple Developer Program: https://developer.apple.com/programs/

Pay particular attention to app completeness, privacy policy, consent/permissions, account deletion, login service requirements, user-generated content, payments, third-party content rights, review access, and correct metadata.

## Google Play

- Developer Program Policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/16893504
- User Data policy: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10144311
- Account deletion requirements: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13327111
- Data Safety: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469
- Play Console Help: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/
- Android Studio system requirements: https://developer.android.com/studio/install

Pay particular attention to user data, privacy policy, Data Safety, account/data deletion, UGC, child safety, permissions, target API, ads, subscriptions/payments, app access/reviewer credentials, and developer verification.
